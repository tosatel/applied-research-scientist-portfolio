# Research Protocol
## Objective
Compare AI systems across multiple trustworthiness dimensions and test whether critical gates reveal weaknesses hidden by aggregate measures.

## Independent Variables
System, dimension weights, critical thresholds.

## Dependent Variables
Dimension scores, composite trust indicator, minimum score, gate status, readiness category.

## Procedure
1. Load normalized evidence.
2. Validate scores are within [0,1].
3. Compute weighted composite indicator.
4. Apply dimension-specific gates.
5. Assign an explicit descriptive readiness category.
6. Inspect weakest dimensions and failures.

## Extensions
Add repeated measurements, confidence intervals, uncertainty, inter-rater reliability, subgroup analysis, and sensitivity analysis.
